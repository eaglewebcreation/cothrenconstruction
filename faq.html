Emergencies + plumbing

Do you take emergency plumbing calls?

Yes. We take emergency plumbing calls 24 hours a day, 7 days a week in Lovell and across the Big Horn Basin. Call (307) 578-7873. For an emergency, a phone call is faster than the form.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Lovell, Wyoming and work across the Big Horn Basin. If you're not sure we come out your way, give us a call and ask.

Do you repair and replace water heaters?

Yes. We repair water heaters and replace them when it's time, including jobs that mean crawling under the house.

Can you fix a backed up sewer or drain line?

Yes. We clear and repair sewer and drain lines, and we make sure everything is working before we leave.

Do you work on old plumbing?

Yes. Updating and rerouting plumbing in older homes is a big part of what we do, often as part of a bathroom or kitchen remodel.

Do you do HVAC?

Yes, heating and cooling work is on our list. Tell us what you've got going on and we'll let you know how we can help.

Building + remodels

Can one crew handle the plumbing and the remodel?

Yes, that's the point of having both trades under one roof. On a bathroom remodel, for example, the same crew does the framing, the plumbing and the finish work.

Do you build new homes and shops?

Yes. We build new homes, new shops, sheds and garages around Lovell and the Big Horn Basin, and we can plumb them too.

Do you do excavation, dirt work and concrete?

Yes. We do excavation, dirt work and concrete, plus irrigation, pumps, sprinklers and custom landscaping.

Do you build and repair decks and fences?

Yes, both new builds and repairs.

Do you rent trailers and equipment?

Yes. We rent trailers and equipment by the day or the week, with easy pickup and drop-off. Ask us about rates.
